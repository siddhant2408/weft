Margin settings

Model

Who replies to your comments. Nothing is sent anywhere until you send a comment or click Summarise.

Provider

The default, openrouter/free, costs nothing but picks a random free model each call, is rate-limited, and free providers may log or train on what you send. Set a paid model (e.g. anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5) for steadier, private replies. The key is sent only to OpenRouter.

Replies count toward your ChatGPT plan's usage, shared with ChatGPT itself, including its 5-hour limit. Set how much Margin may use, or disconnect it, at ChatGPT settings → Usage. Your sign-in is stored in Margin's own extension storage, out of reach of web pages, and sent only to OpenAI. A weekly cap there limits what a leaked sign-in could spend.

Tags and takeaways are short jobs, so a smaller, cheaper model does fine there. Switching the replies model re-sends the article on your next question.

Related threads

Before a reply, Margin looks for threads on other pages that share a tag with this one. With a TypeSafe key, its Jev model scores how much each would help with your question, and only the useful ones (up to 3) are sent along with it. Without a key, the 3 with the most word overlap are sent.

In one request per reply, Jev sees this thread's passage, question and recent messages, plus each candidate's page title, passage, takeaway, last question and last AI reply. Get a key at console.typesafe.ai. The key is sent only to TypeSafe.

Excluded sites

Margin does nothing on these sites: no comment box, no highlights, nothing sent to a model. One domain per line; subdomains are included. Changes apply when you next load a page.

Your data

Everything is stored only in this Chrome profile. Uninstalling Margin deletes it, so export first.
