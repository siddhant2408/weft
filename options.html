Margin settings

Model

Who answers your questions. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click Ask or Summarise.

Provider

API keys are stored in this Chrome profile's extension storage and sent only to the provider you choose.

Excluded sites

Margin does nothing on these sites: no Ask button, no highlights, nothing sent to Claude. One domain per line; subdomains are included. Changes apply when you next load a page.

Your data

Everything is stored only in this Chrome profile. Uninstalling Margin deletes it, so export first.
