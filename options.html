Margin settings

Model

Replies come from OpenRouter, pay per use, any model. Nothing is sent anywhere until you send a comment or click Summarise.

The default, openrouter/free, costs nothing but picks a random free model each call, is rate-limited, and free providers may log or train on what you send. Set a paid model (e.g. anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5) for steadier, private replies. The small model handles quick jobs: concept tags, one-line takeaways and picking related threads. Switching the main model re-sends the article on your next question.

The API key is stored in this Chrome profile's extension storage and sent only to OpenRouter.

Excluded sites

Margin does nothing on these sites: no comment box, no highlights, nothing sent to a model. One domain per line; subdomains are included. Changes apply when you next load a page.

Your data

Everything is stored only in this Chrome profile. Uninstalling Margin deletes it, so export first.
